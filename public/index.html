About Speedline

A Dubai-based trading partner built on trust and consistency.

Speedline General Trading LLC is a U.A.E.-licensed commodities trading house specialising in the global supply of agricultural produce, metals, and industrial commodities. From our base in Dubai — one of the world's busiest trade corridors — we connect producers and refineries across leading origins with buyers across the Middle East, Asia, and Africa.

Trusted origins. Direct relationships with established producers and multi-origin suppliers ensure consistent grade and reliable availability.
Inspected quality. Every shipment moves with SGS-grade inspection and transparent, internationally recognised documentation.
End-to-end logistics. We manage product reliably from origin to destination port — on spec, on schedule, CIF or FOB.
