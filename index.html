Trade & Export

Seamless International Trade, End to End

From contract to port of destination — we manage every step with precision and transparency.

Contract & Compliance

International trade contracts in accordance with ICC rules, covering full terms, payment schedules, and specification guarantees for both buyer and seller protection.

CIF & FOB Delivery

Flexible Incoterms — supply on CIF (cost, insurance & freight) to your port, or FOB from origin. We coordinate maritime bookings, port handling, and transit insurance.

Trade Documentation

Complete shipping documentation package: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, and SGS inspection report.

Payment Terms

Transactions processed via ADCB, Dubai — irrevocable LC at sight, TTL, or DA/DP terms depending on trade history and volume. Transparent banking from day one.

Quality Inspection

Every shipment is independently inspected at origin by SGS or equivalent, with certificates of analysis provided prior to loading to guarantee specification compliance.

Send us your requirement — grade, quantity, destination and preferred Incoterms — and our trade desk will respond with a firm offer within one business day.
